Jaipur: The Historic Pink City

Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, Jaipur is Rajasthan's capital and a UNESCO World Heritage site renowned for magnificent architecture and vibrant traditions.

Palace of Winds (Hawa Mahal)

Constructed in 1799 with 953 intricate honeycombed jharokhas (casements) that allowed royal women to observe street festivals unobserved.

Vibrant Arts, Handlooms & Royal Cuisine

From world-famous blue pottery and block-printed textiles to delectable Dal Baati Churma, the living heritage of Jaipur captivates travelers across the globe.

Majestic Amber Fort & Palace

Perched atop rugged hills overlooking Maota Lake, Amer Fort is a timeless blend of Hindu and Rajput architectural grandeur.
